# Contributing

- Branch from `main`: `feat/<area>-<short-name>` (e.g. `feat/engine-ranking`). Open a PR; one teammate reviews before merge.
- Stay inside your area (see ownership table in README) to avoid conflicts.
- Never commit raw data, secrets or `.env` files.
- Any change touching safety behaviour (group O fallback, lending limits, human approval) needs review from the whole team.
- Keep commits small; during the hackathon, merge early and often.
